ICCE 2024 Outstanding Paper Award & Best Poster Design Award 수상

2025-04-16

At ICCE 2024, Hyo-Jeong So and Sungeun Kim won the Outstanding Paper Award with their paper ‘Dialogue Game-Based Learning for AI Ethics Education’. Also, Lingxi Jin, Kim Kyu-won, and Lee Ga-young won the Best Poster Design Award with their academic poster ‘What do University Students Say About ChatGPT? A Topic Modeling of Perception on GenAI in Academic Writing'.
