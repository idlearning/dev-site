ICLS 2025 & AIED 2025 Main Track 억셉

2025-04-16

6 papers from the IDL Lab have been accepted to the main track of ICLS 2025 and AIED 2025. The accepted papers will be presented at ICLS 2025, held in Helsinki, Finland from June 10 to 13, and at AIED 2025, taking place in Palermo, Italy from July 22 to 26.

📄
FLIP: Tabletop Role-Play Game for AI Ethics Education through Cognitive Dissonance

Full Paper · ICLS 2025

Yue Wang, Yeji Ko, Kyuwon Kim, Sung-Eun Kim, Hyo-Jeong So

📄
Adaptive Scaffolding with Generative AI Chatbot for Solving Ill-Structured Problems

Short Paper · ICLS 2025

Hyeji Jang, Hyo-Jeong So

📄
Global Approaches to AIED: Common Voices and Silences about the Sustainable Use of Generative AI in Education

Poster · ICLS 2025

Hyo-Jeong So, Lynde Tan, Beaumie Kim

📄
Dilemmas in AI Ethics: A Digital Game for Moral Reasoning and Collective Decision-Making

Full Paper · AIED 2025

Sungeun Kim, Kyuwon Kim, Jeanhee Lee, Yeji Ko, Wang Yue, Hyo-Jeong So

📄
Learning by Teaching: Enhancing Music Learning Through LLM-Based Teachable Agents

Short Paper · AIED 2025

Lingxi Jin, Baicheng Lin, Mengze Hong, Hyo-Jeong So, Kun Zhang
