ICoME 2025 Young Scholar Award 수상

2025-08-10

At ICoME 2025, Jeanhee Lee and Yoon-Kyeong Lee won the Young Scholar Award for their paper “Integrating Computational Thinking into Experimental Design-Based Inquiry: A Structured STEM Lesson Using Flowcharts.” The conference, co-hosted by the Korea Association for Educational Information and Media (KAEIM), the Japan Association for Educational Media Studies (JAEMS), the China Association for Educational Technology (CAET), and Teaching, Colleges, and Community (TCC), was held at Inha University from Thursday, August 7 to Friday, August 8, 2025. Their study proposed a middle school science inquiry lesson that integrates computational thinking elements—decomposition, abstraction, algorithmic thinking, and debugging—into experimental design using flowcharts and the Flowgorithm program.
